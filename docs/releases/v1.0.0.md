# Caster's Compendium v1.0.0

v1.0.0 is the first stable **manual-playable networked simulator skeleton**.
It is rules-assisted, not rules-complete: printed card effects remain mostly
manual, while turn structure, battle, Orb corruption, Break, and online lobby
play are enforced enough for real playtests.

## Online lobby and multiplayer

- Browse open rooms, search by name, and create named rooms with optional
  passwords.
- Join as a player or spectator; spectators receive view-only projections.
- Carry display names into the match and choose Arena-style full control or
  stop-phase auto-pass for priority.
- Use protocol version 3 against a matching `simhost` deployment.

## Battle and Orb rules

- Declare attacks (left-click or Battle panel), resolve servant judgment, and
  corrupt Orbs after player attacks.
- Support Double Corrupt (printed or granted), including multi-Orb choice and
  Break queues; Double Corrupt against one Orb does not win the game.
- Right-click a Servant to grant or clear Double Corrupt for caster-style
  temporary grants.
- Block ending the Battle Phase while a Recovered Servant is still able to
  attack.

## Manual Cockatrice-style board ops

- Drag-and-drop and manual moves across supported zones, including Hand→Orb.
- Peek, dig, browse, draw, and shuffle helpers for deck operations.
- Peek/reveal Orbs with persistent owner knowledge where appropriate.
- Sage Advice optional dig-on-draw when controlling face-up Sage Advice.

## Presentation

- Scale simulator and network dialogs with the window size.
- Show a DC badge when a Servant has Double Corrupt (printed or granted).

## Honest limits

- Most printed Enter / Last Words / activated text stays player-driven.
- Slow Start and attack costs are not auto-enforced yet.
- Break offer order after multi-corrupt follows attacker selection order
  (owner reorder UI not added).
