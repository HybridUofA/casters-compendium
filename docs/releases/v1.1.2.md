# Caster's Compendium v1.1.2

v1.1.2 cuts empty Pass Priority click fatigue and adds the first typed
activated-ability automation for Hilde Willow, plus a reusable pending-choice
prompt framework for Hybrid-owned Enter / Last Words work.

## Arena-style priority auto-pass

- Default priority mode auto-passes empty stacks and Chase priority when you
  have no legal non-main-timing response.
- Stops on Chase when your hand has Quickcast, or when a recovered field card
  has a non-aether activated ability (for example Hilde Willow's Rest).
- Aether generation alone does not force a stop (CR 9.7 mana-ability analogue).
- Pending Break, Sage Advice, and generic PendingChoice decisions still stop.
- Turn player still keeps Main / Battle with an empty Chase.
- Preference label updated to match Arena-style wording; Full and stop-phase
  modes are unchanged.

## Hilde Willow Rest ability

- While you hold priority, tap recovered Hilde Willow and choose Produce Aether
  or Look at enemy deck.
- The ability rests her, shows the enemy deck top privately, then offers Leave
  on top or Put on bottom.
- Resolves immediately for now; activated-ability Chase resolution is not
  implemented yet.

## Pending choice framework

- Adds `PendingChoice` match state, viewer projection, session/nethost
  commands, and a shared board decision strip (also used by Sage Advice /
  Break presentation).
- Thin `internal/simulator/effects` registry hooks for Hybrid-owned Enter /
  Last Words handlers.

## Compatibility

Deck files, settings, and protocol version 3 are unchanged. Online play should
use a `simhost` built from this tag (or later) so `activate_hilde_willow` and
pending-choice commands are available on the host.
